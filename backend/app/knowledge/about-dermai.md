# About DermAI and how to use it

## What the DermAI assistant can and cannot do
The DermAI assistant gives general information about skin, hair and nail health from a reviewed knowledge base. It cannot diagnose you, cannot see your skin, and cannot prescribe treatment. For advice about your own skin, book an appointment with a dermatologist.

## AI photo analysis
On the Skin photos page you can upload a photo for an AI preliminary screening. The current model only checks for acne, eczema and fungal infection; it cannot detect other conditions, including skin cancer, and it can be wrong even when its score is high. The heatmap shows which parts of the photo the model focused on. Treat the result as a prompt to talk to a doctor, never as a diagnosis.

## Taking a good photo
Use bright, even natural light without flash, hold the camera steady about 10 to 15 cm from the skin, keep the affected area in sharp focus and fill most of the frame, and include a little normal skin around it for comparison.

## Booking a dermatologist
Go to "Find a doctor" in the patient menu, choose a hospital, pick a dermatologist and book an available time. Your appointments appear under "Appointments", and notes from your visits appear under "Medical records".
