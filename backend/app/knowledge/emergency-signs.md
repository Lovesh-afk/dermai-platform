# Skin symptoms that need urgent medical care

## Call emergency services immediately
Call your local emergency number (112 in India and Europe, 911 in the US) if skin symptoms come with: difficulty breathing or swallowing; swelling of the face, lips, tongue or throat; wheezing, dizziness or fainting after a sting, food or new medicine (possible anaphylaxis); or a rash of purple or dark red spots that do not fade when pressed with a glass, especially with fever or feeling very unwell.

## Get same-day urgent care
Seek urgent care the same day for: skin that is rapidly becoming red, hot, swollen and painful, especially with fever or chills (possible serious skin infection such as cellulitis); widespread blistering or peeling skin, especially with fever or after starting a new medicine; a painful blistering rash near the eye; severe or large burns; or an animal or human bite that broke the skin.

## Severe reactions to medicines
Rarely, medicines can cause severe skin reactions that start with fever and flu-like symptoms followed by a painful red or purple rash, blisters, peeling skin and sores in the mouth, eyes or genitals. This is a medical emergency: stop and seek emergency care immediately.
