# Everyday skin care and sun protection

## Sun protection
Use a broad-spectrum sunscreen of SPF 30 or higher on all exposed skin every day, applied generously about 15 minutes before going outside and reapplied about every two hours and after swimming or sweating. Seek shade in the middle of the day when UV is strongest, wear protective clothing, a wide-brimmed hat and UV-blocking sunglasses, and avoid tanning beds.

## Daily skin care basics
Cleanse gently with a mild, fragrance-free cleanser, avoid very hot water, and moisturise after washing to protect the skin barrier. Introduce new products one at a time so you can tell what causes a reaction. Drink enough water, get enough sleep, and avoid smoking, which speeds up skin ageing.

## Checking your own skin
Look over your whole skin about once a month, including the scalp, palms, soles and between the toes, using a mirror or a helper for hard-to-see areas. Note any new spots or changes to existing moles and show them to a doctor.
